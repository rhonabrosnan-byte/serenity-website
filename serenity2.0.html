About Us

A community nonprofit for adults living with memory loss and the families who love them

Our Story

Serenity was born from the personal experiences of a group of individuals whose families have been touched by cognitive impairment and memory loss. Through our own journeys, we came to understand the challenges faced by individuals living with memory changes, as well as the families and caregivers who support them.

As we explored opportunities for our loved ones to remain socially connected, mentally stimulated, and actively engaged in daily life, we recognized a significant gap in our community: there was no dedicated local program offering the meaningful activities, companionship, and supportive environment these individuals deserve.

Rather than accept that this resource was missing, we came together with a shared vision: to create Serenity—a welcoming place where individuals experiencing memory loss can continue to find purpose, build connections, experience joy, and feel a sense of belonging.

Our Philosophy

A social model, not a medical one

At Serenity, we believe people living with memory loss still have so much to give and so much to enjoy. Our program is built around connection, purpose and joy, not medical treatment.

Each day brings creative, physical and mental activity in a calm, familiar setting, where every participant can take part at their own pace and be seen for who they are.

Our Mission

To enrich the daily lives of individuals with memory loss by providing a safe, stimulating and joyful retreat that offers daily engagement for the participants and peace of mind for their families.

Our Vision

We envision a community where individuals living with memory loss have opportunities to remain engaged, connected, and valued, while their families find reassurance, support, and time to recharge.

Adults with early to middle stage memory loss

Serenity is for adults living with memory loss, whether diagnosed dementia or another type of cognitive impairment, who would benefit from activity and time with others.

FAQ's

Who is Serenity for?

Serenity is for adults with early to middle stage memory loss, whether diagnosed dementia or another type of cognitive impairment, who would enjoy activity and time with others.

Is Serenity a medical program?

No. Serenity follows a social model, not a medical one. We focus on connection, engagement and joy, and do not provide medical care.

Student Volunteers

Connecting generations

Some of the best moments at Serenity happen when generations come together. We welcome high school and younger students to spend time with our participants: talking, playing games, making art and simply keeping them company.

We're working to partner with local chapters of the National Charity League (NCL) and the National Honor Society (NHS) to bring student volunteers to Serenity.
